
# My Favorite Series: Wednesday

## Introduction
My favorite series is **Wednesday**. It is a mystery and comedy series that follows the life of Wednesday Addams as she studies at Nevermore Academy.

## About the Series
*Wednesday* is an interesting series because it combines mystery, supernatural events, friendship, and comedy.

### Main Character
- **Wednesday Addams** – The main character who is intelligent, mysterious, and independent.
- **Enid Sinclair** – Wednesday's colorful and friendly roommate.
- **Xavier Thorpe** – A student with artistic abilities.
- **Tyler Galpin** – A character connected to the mysteries in the story.
